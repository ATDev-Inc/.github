## About Us

ATDev creates innovative, assistive technology that empowers people to live independently, confidently, and without barriers.

Founded by experts in engineering, design, and accessibility, we combine advanced technology with deep understanding of user needs to create solutions that truly make a difference.

We believe that everyone deserves the freedom to live life on their own terms. Our mission is to create innovative assistive technology that removes barriers and empowers independence.

## Our Vision

A world where technology adapts to people, not the other way around. Where independence is accessible to all.

- **Cutting-edge technology** - Leveraging the latest innovations
- **Accessible innovation** - Making independence available to all
